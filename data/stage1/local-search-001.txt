The idea and properties of local search heuristics

The idea of local search heuristics

A heuristic is a technique for approaching hard problems that does not give strong guarantees on the quality of the solution, but is efficient.

Local search heuristics are particular heuristic techniques for solving hard combinatorial optimisation problems that are based on the idea of searching the solution space.

Generally speaking, in a local search approach, we start with an initial feasible solution. In each iteration, we move from the current solution s to another "close" solution s' that is obtained from s by a small and quick modification. Each time, we evaluate the objective function while looking for the best solution.

Properties of local search

Positive properties:

- The approach is universal, meaning that it can be applied to any combinatorial problem.
- Algorithms based on local search are usually fast. More precisely, they have acceptably low, polynomial time complexity.

Negative properties:

- There is no guarantee of finding the global optimum.
- There is usually no guarantee of how much poorer the found solution is than the global optimum.
